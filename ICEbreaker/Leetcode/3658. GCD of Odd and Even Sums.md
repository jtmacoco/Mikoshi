## Problem

You are given an integer `n`. Your task is to compute the **GCD** (greatest common divisor) of two values:

- `sumOdd`: the sum of the smallest `n` positive odd numbers.
    
- `sumEven`: the sum of the smallest `n` positive even numbers.
    

Return the GCD of `sumOdd` and `sumEven`.

---
# Example 1

**Input:** `n = 4`

**Output:** 4

**Explanation:**

- Sum of the first 4 odd numbers `sumOdd = 1 + 3 + 5 + 7 = 16`
- Sum of the first 4 even numbers `sumEven = 2 + 4 + 6 + 8 = 20`

Hence, `GCD(sumOdd, sumEven) = GCD(16, 20) = 4`.


---
# Solution

>[!important] Intuition
> - Can use **Euclidean Algorithm** but there is an easier way
> -  Sum of first $n$ odd numbers = $n^2$ 
> - Sum of first $n$ even numbers = $n(n+1)$
> - Need to find gcd of $(n^2 , n(n+1))$

>[!note] Why sum of odds = $n^2$
> Adding odd numbers one at a time always builds a perfect square:
> $1 = 1^2$, $1+3 = 2^2$, $1+3+5 = 3^2$, $1+3+5+7=4^2$
> Each new odd number is just the "L-shaped" layer that grows the square by one ring.

>[!note] Why sum of evens = $n(n+1)$
> $2+4+6+\dots+2n = 2(1+2+3+\dots+n)$
> and $1+2+\dots+n = \dfrac{n(n+1)}{2}$ (the classic **triangular number** / Gauss formula — pair first+last, second+second-last, etc., each pair sums to $n+1$, and there are $n/2$ pairs)
> Multiplying by the 2 out front cancels the denominator: $2 \cdot \dfrac{n(n+1)}{2} = n(n+1)$

Math behind why it's so simple:
$$
\text{gcd}(n², n(n+1)) = n * \text{gcd}(n, n+1)
$$
so we pulled out an $n$ from the equation

>[!note] Why $\text{gcd}(n, n+1) = 1$
> $n$ and $n+1$ are consecutive integers. Any common divisor of both must also divide their difference, $(n+1)-n = 1$. The only number dividing 1 is 1 — so consecutive integers are always coprime.

Since $n$ and $n+1$ are always consecutive (coprime).  the $\text{gcd} = 1$ which leaves $\text{gcd} = n$

Ex:

| n     | odd sum | seven sum | gcd |
| ----- | ------- | --------- | --- |
| n     | 1       | 2         | 1   |
| 2     | 4       | 6         | 2   |
| 3<br> | 9       | 12        | 3   |
**coprime**:  (also called "relatively prime") means two numbers share **no common factors** other than 1. In other words, their GCD is 1.

```python
    def gcdOfOddEvenSums(self, n: int) -> int:
        return n
```
