Sum of Two Integers

Every column is added at once. a ^ b gives the sum bits without carries, and (a & b) << 1 gives the carries, moved one column left. The loop keeps adding the carries back in until there are none left.

32-bit two's complement, like a C++ int. A carry out of bit 31 is dropped, which is why -1 + 1 ends at 0.
